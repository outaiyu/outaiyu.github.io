Purpose▼
Although recycled papermaking is common, there is little precise information on how handmade recycled papers perform with different writing tools and under varying conditions. This project measures the performance of handmade recycled paper under multiple writing tools.
Background▼
Handmade recycled paper is made by breaking down old paper into fibers, mixing them with water, and spreading the pulp into sheets. The fibers, thickness, and water content affect how the paper interacts with pens and other writing tools. Some papers absorb ink quickly, while others allow smoother writing. Different pens perform differently depending on the paper's texture and thickness.
Data▼
Conclusion▼
In conclusion, handmade papers mainly are very bumpy and got great performance on writing tools (except highlighter) after a long drying time. Specifically to types, notebook paper dried faster than tissue paper while one made with tissue paper's pulp formed quickly.
